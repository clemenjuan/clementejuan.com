About

I am a Research Associate and Doctoral Candidate in Prof. Alessandro Golkar's Chair of Spacecraft Systems at the Technical University of Munich (TUM). Since 2024 my doctoral research has focused on autonomous satellite operations with artificial intelligence: multi-agent reinforcement learning, and cognitive architectures built on large language models that reason, plan and call tools to manage large satellite constellations. I am also exploring joint-embedding predictive architectures (JEPA) as world models for space systems.

Before my PhD I earned a Bachelor's in Aerospace Engineering at the Universitat Politècnica de València (Spain) and a Master's in Aerospace Engineering at TUM, where I also worked as a Research Assistant at the University of the Bundeswehr Munich on distributed real-time hybrid systems. Outside the lab I build AI products as a founder (see Ventures).

Projects

  • PhD · AUTOPS

    AUTOPS framework

    A controlled testbed for comparing satellite operations architectures along three axes: who decides (single agent, centralised, decentralised or hybrid multi-agent), how (rules, reinforcement learning, LLM agents with safety shields, or learned world-model planning) and where (ground, onboard or hybrid).

  • Space mission

    EventSat

    A 6U CubeSat from the Chair of Spacecraft Systems that flies a neuromorphic event camera to detect objects in orbit. I lead the on-board computer and command & data handling (OBC & CDH) subsystem.

    Mission page ›
  • PhD · First-author paper

    Multi-agent RL for constellations

    Decentralised decision-making for space situational awareness under partial observability, trained with multi-agent PPO (EUCASS 2025). Within AUTOPS, these RL policies keep being improved and are benchmarked against rule-based, LLM-agent and world-model planners.

    Project page ›
  • PhD · AUTOPS

    Onboard world models

    Latent world models in the spirit of JEPA, learned from operations data and used for onboard planning, evaluated against an exact-dynamics reference planner.

  • PhD · AUTOPS

    Calibrated decision models

    Can a small, fast model choose what a satellite does next, and know when it is unsure? I adapt calibrated decision models to onboard operations, training them on simulated mission outcomes so their confidence can gate when the spacecraft acts on its own.
