About

I am a Research Associate and Doctoral Candidate in Prof. Alessandro Golkar's Chair of Spacecraft Systems at the Technical University of Munich (TUM). Since 2024 my doctoral research has focused on autonomous satellite operations with artificial intelligence: multi-agent reinforcement learning, and cognitive architectures built on large language models that reason, plan and call tools to manage large satellite constellations. I am also exploring joint-embedding predictive architectures (JEPA) as world models for space systems.

Before my PhD I earned a Bachelor's in Aerospace Engineering at the Universitat Politècnica de València (Spain) and a Master's in Aerospace Engineering at TUM, where I also worked as a Research Assistant at the University of the Bundeswehr Munich on distributed real-time hybrid systems. Outside the lab I build AI products as a founder (see Ventures).

Research interests

  • Autonomous constellations

    Decentralised decision-making for large satellite constellations under partial observability.

  • Multi-agent RL

    Reinforcement learning for coordinated satellite tasking, e.g. space situational awareness.

  • Agentic spacecraft operations

    LLM-based agent architectures for reasoning, planning and tool calling in mission operations.

  • Space world models

    Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPA) to learn predictive models of spacecraft and their environment.
